Hello, I'm Junyoung Yun, a full-cycle 3D computer vision engineer.

I earned my master's degree researching object recognition and integration for autonomous driving. As a Technical Research Personnel (전문연구요원), I now work on the research and development of 3D computer vision. Having experienced the full cycle from research to productization, I have built not only a broad understanding spanning traditional 3D techniques to deep-learning-based 3D vision, but also the ability to realize them as real products that interact with the physical world through hardware.

I pursue living technology that delivers real value to users rather than research for its own sake, and I strive to become an ‘Empowered Engineer’ who can proactively define and solve problems.
